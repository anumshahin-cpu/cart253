### September 24 2026

I thought the activity was difficult but very fun to do. I couldn't help but feel a bit frustrated at times when I couldn't get the variables right or whenever I ran into errors, but seeing my work at the end always made it worth it. For my prototypes, I decided to do a key for my simple prototype, a close up of a four leaf clover for my abstract prototype, and a character named Chiikawa for my "really weird" prototype (even though Chiikawa isn't weird, but cute!). Originally I had asked for some advice on my four leaf clover and was given some suggestions, but after working on it and seeing how it looked with the suggestions, I decided that it looked fine as is and wanted to keep it how I submitted it. I thought it would be boring to do just a four leaf clover, so I decided to make it look like it was zoomed in by using four triangles and a circle in the middle so it could give the illusion of rounded edges, when in reality it was just four dark green triangles and a lighter green background. It worked out in my favour and I was really pleased with it. For the key, I thought it would be simple enough but still a bit challenging, and it was. I struggled a lot with placing the tips of the key because I kept experimenting with the different placements, but by the end, I got the hang of it. Same goes for my third prototype, it actually took me the least amount of time despite it having the most amount of things to draw, but I was happy it took a shorter amount of time because I didn't have to struggler for too long!
